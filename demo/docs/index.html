What if every MIDI note knew how loud to play?

A piano-trained velocity estimator meets new instruments. Diff-SFProxy adapts it through two note-wise acoustic cues from a SoundFont, so the estimated dynamics follow the performance.

03 / THE IDEA

Supervise the control, not every waveform detail.

Only VeloEst changes during target-instrument adaptation. The differentiable backends remain frozen.
